What Pig Pal is

Pig Pal is a record-keeping app for families. It tracks chores, allowance, goals and the balances a parent chooses to attribute to a child.

It is not a bank and it does not hold, move or transfer money. There is no account, no card, no payment rail. A balance in Pig Pal is a note of what a parent has decided they owe a child, kept between them. Actual money changes hands in the real world, or it doesn't.

Sharing with other people

Inviting someone to your family gives them full access to it: every child's name, photo, balance and history, and the ability to change any of it. Only invite people you would hand your unlocked phone to.
