Money that means something

Pig Pal is the chore chart on the fridge and the jar on the shelf, kept honest by a ledger that can't be fudged and a parent who approves every penny.

  • Chores that payA kid ticks it off, a parent approves, the money lands — and the streak keeps going.
  • Allowance on a scheduleSet it once. It arrives every week even if nobody opens the app.
  • Goals worth saving forKids split their allowance toward the thing they actually want, and watch it fill.
  • Rewards that aren't moneyA day out for a month of chores — together, or each on their own.
  • Both parents, in stepPrivate iCloud sync. Approve on your phone, and it's already done on theirs.
  • A device each, or one to shareHand a kid a phone and it opens to their screen and nowhere else. The family iPad asks who's here.

Privacy, honestly

Pig Pal collects nothing. There's no Pig Pal account, no Pig Pal server, and no analytics or advertising code anywhere in the app.

Your family's data stays on your devices. Turn on sync and it goes to your iCloud, where we can't reach it — not to us.
