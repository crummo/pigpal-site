Pig Pal

The piggy bank, all grown up. Chores, allowance and goals — in one happy little app for iPhone and iPad.

  • Chores that payA parent approves, the money lands, the streak keeps going.
  • Allowance on a scheduleSet it once and it arrives every week, even if nobody opens the app.
  • Goals worth saving forKids split their allowance toward the thing they actually want.
  • Rewards that aren't moneyA day out for a month of chores, together or each.
  • Both parents, in stepPrivate iCloud sync between your family's devices.
  • A device eachHand a kid a phone and it opens to their screen, and nowhere else.
